# Figure and table source map

All maps and time series are generated by `scripts/plot_figures.py`. Helpers are in `plot_common.py` and `plot_regions.py`. Units are specified by plot labels and NetCDF attributes; `data/fields.json` lists every field variable and dimension.

| Display | Purpose | Primary packaged sources |
|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 | Track recovery in both models and the ERA5T-start comparison | `tracks.json`, `reanalysis_tracks.json` |
| Figure 2 | Initial moist energy and eastern circulation change | `energy.nc`, `initial_fields.nc` |
| Figure 3 | Error growth, ridge position, and meridional steering | `diagnostics.json`, `ridge.json`, `steering.json` |
| Figure 4 | TianXing circulation at +120 h | `tianxing_*_120.nc`, `steering.json` |
| Figure S1 | Reference-center agreement | `tracks.json` → `reference_results` |
| Figure S2 | Signed wind, humidity, temperature, and height increments | `initial_fields.nc` |
| Figure S3 | Broad regional retention | `regional_tracks.json`, `tracks.json`, `energy.nc` |
| Figure S4 | First-day circulation response | `early_fields.nc` |
| Figure S5 | Resolved vorticity-error budget and advection components | `vorticity_budget.json`, `advection.json` |
| Figure S6 | Diagnostic-domain sensitivity | `budget_regions.json` |
| Figure S7 | Pangu-Weather circulation at +120 h | `pangu_6h_*_120.nc`, `steering.json` |
| Figure S8 | Four wind samples and symmetric position/field decomposition | `diagnostics.json` → `steering` |
| Early-budget supporting table | Projection, spatial alignment, and remainder amplitude | `early_budget.json`, `tables/early_budget.csv` |
| Steering supporting table | Both wind fields sampled at both centers | `tables/steering.csv`, filtered to +120 h |

File paths in the table are relative to `data/` except where a `tables/` prefix is shown. Full-precision track scores and time-dependent coordinates are in `tables/track_scores.csv` and `tables/tracks.csv`.

The vorticity scale is 10−5 s−1. `K_scaled` and cumulative error-budget contributions have units (10−5 s−1)². Early transport projections are normalized pattern projections; they are not percentages of independent physical causes. `control_optimal` in the four-sample data means the control wind field sampled at the optimal storm center. Each prefix pair always follows field first, center second.
